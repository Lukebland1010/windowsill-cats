    How it works
    1. One cat in every row.
    2. One cat in every column.
    3. One cat in every coloured patch.
    4. Cats never touch, not even at the corners.

    Start from the starter cat with the lock. It is already in the right spot, so it rules out its whole row, column, patch and every square touching it.

    Tap once to mark a square with × — that is just your own note, it costs nothing. Tap twice on the same square to sit a cat there. Tap a cat to pick it back up. A cat in the wrong square costs a heart. Lose all three and the board is lost: try it again or take a new one.

    Drag to mark: slide your finger across the board and every empty square it crosses gets an ×. Start the drag on an × and it rubs marks out instead. A drag keeps to the row or column it sets off along, so it will not spill into the next one (you can switch that off in Settings). Dragging never places a cat, so it can never cost a heart.

    Auto ×: when a cat sits down, the squares it rules out get a faint × on their own. Those squares cannot take a cat, so tapping one does nothing and costs nothing. You can switch this off in Settings.

    Hint points at the next square you can work out and tells you why. Tap it again to sit the cat there.

    Difficulty: Normal gives you extra starter cats, Hard gives you one, Expert gives you none.

    Daily board: the Daily puzzle is the same for everyone. If you are signed in, your first solve time of the day goes on the shared board under the puzzle. Restarting is free, but only that first solve counts.

    Bonus boards show up after every fourth level. One heart, no hints, and a rarer cat if you clear it. Always optional, and they never block your next level.

    Every puzzle is generated fresh and has exactly one solution.
    No ads, no lives to buy. Plays offline.

    Play

    Single tap places a cat Off: tap once for ×, twice for a cat. On: tap once for a cat, tap twice quickly for ×, tap an × to clear it. Faster, but a mis-tap costs a heart.
    Forgiving hearts Off: run out of hearts and the board is lost, so you start it over. On: hearts refill for free and you keep your progress.
    Drag to mark × Slide a finger across the board to mark every square it crosses. Start on an × to rub marks out instead. While this is on, the page does not scroll from inside the board.
    Straight drags A drag stays in the row or column it sets off along, so a wobbly finger cannot leave stray marks next door. Off: marks follow your finger anywhere, diagonals included.
    Auto × When a cat sits down, every square it rules out gets a faint × for you: its row, column, patch and the squares touching it. Your own × marks are never touched.
    Post my daily time Shares your first solve time each day, with your name, on the Daily board. Only people you have shared this page with can see it.
